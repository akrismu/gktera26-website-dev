# History of GKJTU

The GKJTU (Greja Kristian Jawa Tangan Utara)

In the mid-19th century, amidst the lush landscapes of Simo, Boyolali a single family ignited a movement that would transform their community and lay the foundation for the North Central Java Christian Church (GKJTU). The story begins with D.D. Le Jolle, a plantation entrepreneur, and his family, who embraced the gospel with fervor, not just within their household but extending their faith to their employees and the surrounding village. This initial act of faith and evangelization set in motion a journey of growth, challenge, and unwavering belief.
