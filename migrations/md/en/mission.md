# Vision & Mission

GKJTU strives for continuous growth.

## Mature
- Recognizing the difference between right and wrong then doing what is right (Hebrews 5:14)
- Not easily swayed by the teachings and views of others (Ephesians 4:13-16)
- Rooted in love (Ephesians 4:13-16) - Words and actions and church organization policies that 'flow from love'
- Serving the church (Ephesians 4:13-16) - Congregation members able to find their place to serve and encourage one another.
- Living with an eternal perspective (Philippians 3:13-15) - Consciously striving to nurture continuous spiritual growth because they know this life is temporary.
- Controlling their tongue (James 3:2)
- Reflecting God's character to the world (Matthew 5:48)

## Independent
Having the initiative and alternative options to achieve independence:
- Theology
- Human resources
- Funding sources to support the effectiveness and acceleration of the implementation of the Church's Triple Calling

## Mission
The growth of systemic movement within congregations and the GKJTU organization, to become a vehicle for the realization of:
- The Mission of God's Love
- The expansion of the joyous news to all creation
- Humanizing Humanity
- Creating spaces for dialogue across works, cultures, faiths, and religions, as well as a binder for the integrity in a diverse society

## Strategy plan
Long-term goals are crucial for the implementation of progress. GKJTU recognizes this and is working on long-term plans up to 2028:
- Developing personal capacity, inter-congregational relations, and organizational to realize the church's mature character;
- Developing initiatives to achieve theological independence, resources, and funding;
- Developing and multiplying congregational movements to engage in realizing peace, harmony, justice, and prosperity in society
